**Sound is no longer just ambience - it shapes your survival.**

Every footstep, every broken block, every arrow fired sends ripples through the world. Mobs hear these sounds and react. Some will investigate from far away. Others will call friends to join the hunt. Being quiet isn't just nice to have anymore - it's how you stay alive.

---

## What It Does

This mod gives mobs actual hearing. They can:
- **Detect sounds from blocks away** - not just the usual sight range
- **Prioritize louder threats** - breaking glass gets more attention than walking
- **Work together** - one zombie hearing you can alert the whole group
- **Forget and investigate** - lose their target but search the last known position

The result is combat and stealth that feels more alive. Crouching actually matters. Building loudly at night brings danger. Gunfights turn into frantic escapes as enemies converge from all directions.

---

## Works With Your Favorite Mods

- **Point Blank / TaCZ** - Guns attract the right amount of attention based on caliber and suppressors
- **CS Grenades** - Flashbangs blind mobs, smoke blocks their line of sight
- **Enhanced AI** - Respects X-ray targeting configs
- **Custom NPCs** - Guards respond to alarms and intruders
- **Simple Voice Chat** - Talking too loud near mobs? They'll hear you.

---

## Configuration

Everything lives in `config/soundattract/`. You can:
- Tune which mobs care about sounds and how much
- Set custom detection ranges for specific sounds
- Balance stealth mechanics to your pack
- Adjust voice chat detection sensitivity
